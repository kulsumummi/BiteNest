About BiteNest

Online Food Ordering Platform designed for speed, simplicity, and great dining experiences.

Our Mission

BiteNest was built with a simple goal: to connect hungry customers with local restaurants through a seamless, hassle-free online ordering experience.

Key Highlights

  • Clean, intuitive food ordering interface with instant search and category filters.
  • Transparent order tracking from Pending to Delivered status.
  • Hassle-free demo checkout with Cash on Delivery options.
  • Dedicated admin dashboard for restaurant owners and platform managers.

Technology Stack

Built using Python, Django, SQL (SQLite / PostgreSQL), HTML5, and CSS3 without unnecessary heavy dependencies or complex JavaScript frameworks.
